Accounts and AdMob

Do these first. Step 4 is the only thing that blocks build 11.

  1. developer.apple.com/account, then App Store Connect > Business

    Sign in as the Account Holder. If an updated Apple Developer Program License Agreement waits (Apple posted one on October 13), accept it. In Business, the Paid Apps agreement should already be Active from Capi; confirm it and accept anything marked pending. Without Paid Apps, Remove Ads cannot be bought, even in TestFlight.

    Done when: No agreement waits for you, and Paid Apps says Active.

  2. AdMob > Apps > Add app

    Platform: iOS. When AdMob asks if the app is listed on a supported app store, answer No: it is not live yet, and you link it after approval (step 26). App name:

    App name
    El Corre Corre

    Then open the new app > Blocking controls > Ad content rating: turn off Match account-level setting and choose PG. The game already asks Google for PG ads only, which fits the expected 9+ rating.

    Done when: El Corre Corre is listed in AdMob with ad content rating PG.

  3. AdMob > Apps > El Corre Corre > Ad units > Add ad unit

    First a Banner unit (it serves the anchored adaptive banner on the pause screen and Los Mandaos). Leave the advanced settings as they are.

    Banner unit name
    Banner pausa y mandaos

    Then a Rewarded unit. Reward amount 1, reward item below. The game ignores these two values: it doubles the run’s plátanos by itself.

    Rewarded unit name
    Rewarded duplica platanos
    Reward item
    platanos

    Done when: The app shows two ad units: one Banner, one Rewarded.

  4. AdMob > Apps > El Corre Corre > App settings, then each ad unit

    App settings shows the App ID, shaped ca-app-pub-4879291425090726~0000000000 (with a ~). Each ad unit shows its ad unit ID, shaped ca-app-pub-4879291425090726/0000000000 (with a /). Paste all three in the chat with Claude, labeled app, banner and rewarded. Claude puts them in src/adIds.ts; the release build refuses to run until they are there, so test ads can never ship.

    Done when: Claude confirms the ids are in and the release build passes.

  5. AdMob > Privacy & messaging

    European regulations (GDPR): create a message for El Corre Corre, languages Spanish and English, and press Publish. IDFA explainer: create one for El Corre Corre and press Publish. A US state regulations message is optional. Unpublished messages never show: players in the EU and UK would then get no ads.

    Done when: Both messages show Published for El Corre Corre.

  6. App Store Connect > Apps > El Corre Corre > Monetization > In-App Purchases > (+)

    Create it, then on its page: Availability > all countries and regions > Save. Price Schedule > USD 1.99 > Save. App Store Localization > add both languages > Save each. Review Information > the screenshot and the notes > Save. Tax category: leave the default. Family Sharing: your call (it cannot be turned off once on).

Test on your iPhone

On build 11 from TestFlight. If anything fails, stop and send Claude a screenshot.

  1. Your iPhone, TestFlight > El Corre Corre

    Delete El Corre Corre, install build 11 from TestFlight, and open it. About a second after the title appears, the iOS tracking prompt shows over the title (after Google’s message, if your region gets one). It never opens during a run. Tap Ask App Not to Track: ads still work, just not personalized.

    Done when: The prompt showed once, over the title screen.

  2. In the game

    Pause a run: a banner sits at the bottom with every control clear above it. Open MANDAOS on the title: banner there too. The title, the run, the crash and the game-over card have no banner. Collect plátanos and crash: the ×2 ANUNCIO button appears on the plátanos row about a second after the card lands. Watch the video to the end: the plátanos double once and the music comes back. Close one early on another run: nothing is added. Tap the AdChoices icon on an ad and note what it offers.

    Done when: Banner only on pause and Los Mandaos; the video doubles plátanos once.

  3. Title screen > AD button under the sound button

    The sheet shows the App Store price. Buy it (TestFlight purchases charge nothing): ¡Listo! Se fueron los banners. The pause banner is gone at once, the AD button disappears, and the ×2 video still works. Delete the app, reinstall from TestFlight, open the sheet from PAUSA > Quitar anuncios, tap Restaurar compras: Compra restaurada, and the banners stay gone.

    Done when: Bought, banners gone, restored after a reinstall.

  4. Control Center > Airplane mode

    Turn on Airplane mode, close the app fully, and open it. No ×2 button, no banner, no error, and the game plays as before. Turn Airplane mode off.

    Done when: A full run works in Airplane mode.

  5. Game-over card > COMPARTIR

    Share a card to WhatsApp and to Messages, then Guardar imagen: the Spanish photo prompt shows and the card lands in Fotos. Then play 2 runs, buy a pintura, force-quit the app, and reopen: plátanos, the pintura, your records and Remove Ads are all still there.

    Done when: Shares work, and nothing is lost after a force-quit.

  6. In the game

    Swipe up from the FRENO and ¡DALE! area: the first swipe only shows the home bar. Sound starts after the first tap; you feel taps on near-misses, pickups and crashes. Take a phone call mid-run: the game pauses, and the 3, 2, 1 countdown resumes it.

    Done when: Pedals, sound, haptics and pause all behave.

    Notes
    El Corre Corre is an arcade game that plays fully offline. There is no account, no login and no server of our own. Tap the road on the title screen (it reads TOCA PA’ EMPEZAR in Spanish) to start a run.
Tracking and consent: about one second after the title screen first appears, the App Tracking Transparency prompt shows over the title screen. Where Google’s consent rules apply (EEA, UK, Switzerland), Google’s consent message shows first. Prompts never open during a run. Ads work with either answer; without tracking permission the game requests non-personalized ads only.
Ads (Google AdMob): a small banner appears only on the pause screen and on the missions list (MANDAOS on the title screen). There are no interstitials and no ads during play. After a run that collected plantains, the game-over card offers an optional rewarded video: the ×2 button marked "anuncio" (Spanish for ad) on the plantains row. It plays only when tapped and doubles the plantains of that run. Every ad carries Google’s AdChoices icon, where players can report an ad; the privacy policy also gives a contact email for reports.
In-app purchase: Remove Ads ($1.99, non-consumable) removes the banners. The optional video stays because it plays only on request. Open it from the "AD" button under the sound button on the title screen, or from "Quitar anuncios" (Remove ads) on the pause screen. Restore Purchases is in the same sheet.
Pause screen: start a run and tap the pause button at the top left. It holds Remove ads, Privacy (the privacy policy) and, where Google’s consent rules apply, "Privacidad de anuncios" (Ad privacy), which reopens Google’s consent choices.
Controls: drag anywhere to steer. The two pedals at the bottom left are GAS (faster, points x1.5) and BRAKE (slower, points x0.5; it overheats if held for about 2 seconds). Swipe up and hold for a wheelie. Riding in the oncoming lanes doubles points.
Missions (Los Mandaos) are in-game goals in sets of three, plus one daily goal. Paint jobs are bought only with plantains collected in runs, earned from missions or doubled by the optional video. Plantains cannot be bought with money.
SHARE on the game-over card opens the iOS share sheet with an image of the run. The game sends nothing by itself.
Spanish is the default. Tap EN at the top right of the title screen for English.
