# PLAYTEST NOW

Living log of what the human should feel-test at the end of each phase.
Run it with `npm run dev`. Desktop: A/D or arrows to steer, HOLD W or Space
for el caballito, M for mute. Touch: drag to steer, swipe up and hold for the
caballito, swipe down to drop it.

## Phase 5: juice, vehicles, retention (current)

1. **El caballito nuevo.** Hold it as long as you dare: steering decays and
   the bike wobbles the deeper you go, but EVERYTHING pays x1.5 while it
   lasts, stacking with contra via (x3 total). Does the risk/reward tempt you
   into holding it one beat too long? That is the design. Knobs:
   `wheelieMultiplier`, `wheelieMaxSec`, `wheelieSteerFactorEnd` in
   [src/config.ts](src/config.ts).
2. **The three rides.** La Pasola (small, nimble, capped speed), El Motor
   (balanced), El Civic Tuneao (+15% speed and score, fat hitbox, heavy
   steering, underglow, head out the sunroof). Do they FEEL different within
   ten seconds each? Does the Civic's extra score justify its clumsiness?
3. **Difficulty.** Ramp steepened and density raised per your note. Does
   minute two now demand real attention? Does it ever tip into unfair?
4. **"¡Te quedaste a N!"** Die close to your record and watch for the red
   line on the card. Does it make you hit OTRA VEZ before thinking?
5. **The dembow at 120.** Faster, hats on every 16th, the ra-ta ghost snares.
   Does it gallop now instead of swaying?
6. **Share card.** COMPARTIR on the game-over card: on the phone it should
   open the share sheet with a flag-colored score card; on desktop it
   downloads the PNG. Would you actually send this to the group chat?
7. **Juice.** FOV widens with speed, tire dust when carving hard, cartoon
   stars on the crash, shake scaled to severity, haptics on Android web.
